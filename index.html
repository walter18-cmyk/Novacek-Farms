Easy Pinto Bean Recipes

Start with a basic pot of beans, then transform them into quick, family‑friendly meals.

Basic Pot of Pintos
  1. Rinse 2 cups dried beans. Soak 6–8 hours (or quick‑soak by boiling 2 min, rest 1 hour).
  2. Drain. Add fresh water (about 3× bean volume), onion, bay leaf. Bring to a boil.
  3. Simmer gently 60–90 minutes until tender. Salt near the end.

Tip: Cook extra; they freeze well in 1–2 cup portions.

Refried Beans
  1. Warm 2 tbsp oil in a skillet. Add 2 cups cooked pintos with some liquid.
  2. Mash to your preferred texture; season with salt, cumin, and garlic.
  3. Finish with a splash of lime.

Tip: Spread on tortillas, top with cheese, and crisp on a griddle.

Hearty Chili
  1. Brown ground beef or skip for vegetarian. Sauté onion and peppers.
  2. Add tomatoes, spices, and 3 cups cooked pintos. Simmer 20–30 minutes.
  3. Adjust seasoning; serve with cornbread.

Risk Notice & Food Information

We want our food to be safe, delicious, and clearly labeled. Please review before purchasing.

Allergens & Cross‑Contact
  • Product: 100% organic pinto beans. Naturally free of the top 9 allergens.
  • Processed and bagged on our farm with equipment dedicated to dry beans and small grains.
  • Despite careful cleaning, trace cross‑contact with other field crops (corn, wheat, oats, soybeans) is possible.
Preparation & Food Safety
  • Cook thoroughly. Soak and boil until tender (typically 60–90 minutes after soaking).
  • Store dried beans in a cool, dry place; use an airtight container after opening.
  • Small objects hazard: Inspect beans and rinse before cooking; keep uncooked beans away from young children.
Quality & Variability
  • Beans are an agricultural product; minor variation in size or color is natural and safe.
  • Occasional field debris may occur; please sort and rinse prior to cooking.
